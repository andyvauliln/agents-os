## The owner's questions

### 2026-10-05: Does a question and its answer update the knowledge?

> telll me for example i have question you answer me do we update any knowledge from that?

Yes. The question and its answer are stored together as one owner input, word for word, and are recorded in the Questions file of the item the question is about. If the answer adds something new, that also goes into the docs, the decisions and the changelog, and into the metadata of the items it touches. The answer is also written into the doc where the owner should have found it.
