# subagents.md

## Summary

The index of every sub-agent at every level: its unique name, level and file path, role (pre-analysis, self-improvement, support), input, output, memory, the job that runs it, its route and who uses it. Today it lists the system's self-improvement and knowledge base agents, the self-improvement agents of the levels below, and the `news-digest` example, plus retired names kept so they are never reused.

## Keep in mind

- When you add a sub-agent, check its name is unique here and in the agents registry, then give it a row.
