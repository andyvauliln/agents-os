# Questions: vision.md

## Open questions for the owner

- **Which numbers decide success for a trading agent?** Profit and loss, win rate, the deepest fall from a peak, calibration and cost are the candidates. The answer sets what self-improvement aims for and when a version wins.
- **What are the capital limits for the first live accounts?** They set how much a mistake can cost when real money starts.
- **What does the order stop switch do with open positions?** Closing positions needs orders while the switch refuses new ones; the vision cannot state the stop rule until this is settled.
- **May a strategy fold a winning change into itself, or retire losing versions, without the owner?** It decides how fast strategies improve and how much the owner reviews.
- **Which trading domain comes next, and when?** Copy trading was named as the next domain; the vision lists it only as an example until the owner asks for it.
- **How should trading agents and strategies be named on disk?** The names in use are `strategy-1-agent/pm-strategy-1.momentum-v1.opus55-test/`; the stage-one request wrote `strategy-1/strategy-1.v1.Claude-Opus5.5/`. The vision describes the current form until the owner chooses.
- **Through which channel does the owner get trade alerts?** The vision says alerts exist but not how they arrive.
- **Should the order executor and the single ledger become part of the design?** Both are listed as ideas being weighed.
