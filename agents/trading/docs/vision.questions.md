# Questions: vision.md

## Open questions for the owner

- **Which numbers decide the success of a trading agent?** The candidates are profit and loss, win rate, the largest fall from a peak, calibration and cost. The answer sets the target of self-improvement. It also sets when a version wins.
- **What are the capital limits for the first live accounts?** They set the maximum cost of a mistake when real money starts.
- **What does the order stop switch do with open positions?** To close positions, the system must send orders, but the switch refuses new orders. The vision cannot give the stop rule before this is decided.
- **Can a strategy add a winning change to itself, or retire losing versions, without the owner?** The answer decides how fast the strategies improve. It also decides how much the owner must examine.
- **Which trading domain comes next, and when?** An earlier input named copy trading as the next domain. The vision shows it only as an example until the owner asks for it.
- **Which names do the trading agents and the strategies have on disk?** The current names are `strategy-1-agent/pm-strategy-1.momentum-v1.opus55-test/`. The stage-one request used `strategy-1/strategy-1.v1.Claude-Opus5.5/`. The vision uses the current form until the owner decides.
- **Through which channel does the owner get the trade alerts?** The vision tells that alerts exist. It does not tell how they come.
- **Do the order executor and the single ledger become part of the design?** Today they are ideas that we examine.
