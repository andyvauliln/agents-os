# research/

## Summary

The domain's research history: what was asked, what was found and what was decided. `index.json` lists every item with its question, status, results summary, decisions and what it led to, and each item has its own `[research-id]-[slug]/` folder with a README and its artifacts. The domain self-improvement sub-agent writes most items, the owner, the domain agent and support agents add some, and research on one strategy's variants usually sits at that strategy's level, reached through `subagents.link/`. The earlier studies, the prediction-market research among them, now sit in `agents/trading/researches/`. The fields are proposed.

## Keep in mind

- When you start research, read `index.json` first, and keep research here, never in `data/`.
