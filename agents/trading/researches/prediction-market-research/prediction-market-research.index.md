# prediction-market-research/

## What it is

Forty-two studies of ways to make money on prediction markets, numbered `01` to `34` and `50` to `57`, one Markdown file each, such as market making, arbitrage across venues, trading on news and sports feeds, forecasting models and copy trading; some describe practices to avoid. `_evidence/` holds the background evidence (`E1` to `E9`) with the strategy lists, and `_pipeline/` the scripts and notes that produced the studies. The first Polymarket strategy is meant to be chosen from here.

## Who looks after it

It is written by the Polymarket strategy survey sessions (2026-09-20 to 2026-09-29) and `agents/trading/researches/prediction-market-research/_pipeline/stage2-write-verify.js`.

## When and how it changes

It changes when a survey session writes the next batch of studies (58 to 84 are briefed but not written) or runs stage 3 (overview, ranking, conclusions).

## Who uses it, and when

It is read by the owner; knowledge-base-agent (when it writes the trading docs); and the prediction-market domain agent, when it chooses the first strategy (stage two).
