A study of how trading agents are built: a `README.md`, nine architecture specs in `specs/`, their diagrams in `diagrams/` and a gallery page `index.html`; background for the prediction-market domain's trading-agent design. The folder name is kept as it is in the repository.

It is written by the trading-agent architecture research session and the owner (moved it here with the research folder).

## When and how it changes

It changes when an architecture is added, or a diagram is rendered or checked again.

It is read by the owner; knowledge-base-agent (when it writes the trading docs); and the prediction-market domain agent, when it designs its trading agents (stage two).
