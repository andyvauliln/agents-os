(That came through in the Russian keyboard layout, so I'm reading it as "how".) Here's how to use dictation with the page:

- **iPhone or iPad:** tap the Send box, then tap the mic icon on the keyboard. If it's missing, turn on Settings › General › Keyboard › Enable Dictation.
- **Android:** tap the Send box, then tap the mic on the Gboard keyboard.
- **Mac:** click in the Send box and press Fn (🌐) twice. If nothing happens, turn it on in System Settings › Keyboard › Dictation.
- **Windows:** click in the Send box and press Win+H.
