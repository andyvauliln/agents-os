# [plan].md

## Summary

One plan for one change request, written for the owner in plain words: what was asked, what it is for, what exists today, the change part by part with real examples, the choices with a recommended default, the steps with a check for each, what it touches, where its knowledge goes once built, and a log of what happened. It is named by the request's date, time and subject; plans written before the skill keep their old names. It stays a proposal until the owner says go, and is archived once its knowledge is in its homes.

## Keep in mind

- When a step changes while you build, change the plan and say why in its log.
