# record-research.md

## Summary

The runbook for recording a research item at any level, so no work is repeated and every result can be traced. It adds the item to the level's `research/index.json` as planned, creates its folder with a `README.md` holding the question and method, keeps every artifact there, closes it as done or abandoned with results and decisions, and links what it led to: new agents, their `changes.md`, tests, the decision log. The steps are still proposed.

## Keep in mind

- When you finish research, keep the results in the item's folder; the self-improvement helper saves only a pointer in its memory.
