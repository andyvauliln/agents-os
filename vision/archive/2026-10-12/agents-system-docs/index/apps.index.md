# apps.md

## Summary

The list of every app in `apps/`: our own, the outside apps we use and the research clones. Each row gives the app's kind, its source, our fork and its branches, the commit in use, when it was last updated from the source, and which agents use it. Whoever adds, updates or removes an app keeps its row current, and the UI lists from it. Its table format is still a proposal.

## Keep in mind

- When an app is updated from its source, write the new commit and the date in its row.
