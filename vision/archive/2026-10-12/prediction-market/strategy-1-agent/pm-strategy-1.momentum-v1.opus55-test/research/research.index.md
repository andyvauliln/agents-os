# research/

## Summary

The agent's research history: what was asked, what was found and what was decided, so that no research is repeated. Every level has the same layout. `index.json` [50.1] holds one entry per item, with its question, status, results summary, decisions and the variants, changes and tests it led to. Each item also gets a folder [50.2] with a `README.md` and its artifacts. The level's SI sub-agent writes most of it; the owner, domain and strategy agents and support agents may add items too. The UI shows the index. Research on a strategy's variants usually lives at the strategy level instead. The fields are proposed. Still open: whether a backtest is a test, a research item or both.

## Keep in mind

- When you start research, read `index.json` first so you do not repeat work.
- When you keep research, put it here, not in `data/`; memory holds only a pointer and a one-line lesson.
