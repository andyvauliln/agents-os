# research/

## Summary

The strategy's research history: what was asked, found and decided, for the strategy and its variants; research on the variants usually lives here. `index.json` lists every item and its outcome, and each item has a folder with a `README.md` and its artifacts. The self-improvement sub-agent is the main writer: it reads the index before new work, opens an item for one proposed change, links the test variant it creates, and closes the item with the comparison verdict and the fold-back or retirement. The owner and the strategy agent can request items too, and the UI shows the index. Backtests may live here, but only forward test results decide. `subagents.link/` shows each variant's own research.

## Keep in mind

- When you weigh a backtest, treat it as research only; promotions are decided on forward results from test runs.
